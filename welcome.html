AI Email Writer

Pin the extension to keep AI replies one click away.

Click the extensions icon marked as 1, find AI Email Writer, and pin it with the icon marked as 2.

Open Gmail, start a reply, and click Write with AI in the composer.

Once pinned, open Gmail, start a reply or new draft, and use the Write with AI button marked as 3.

Privacy notice: this extension sends account, usage, billing, and AI generation requests to its backend to manage trial limits, checkout, subscription status, and drafted replies.
